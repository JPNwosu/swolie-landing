The film

29 seconds of swole.

Your coach

A buddy for
every set.

swolie rides along in every workout. Before a set it gives you a goal based on last time. After it, a cheer for every rep you beat, and a heads-up the moment you're ready for more.

  • A goal for every set: "you got 8 last time, go for 9"
  • Reactions, PR shout-outs and warm-up checks
  • A guided tour on your very first workout

Smart progression

Hit your reps.
Level up.

Pick a rep range and swolie handles the math. Beat last time set by set, hit the top of the range on every set, and swolie tells you it's time to go heavier. One tap loads the new weight.

  • Auto progression, on from day one
  • Always shows what unlocks the next weight
  • Warm-ups never mess with your numbers

Heart rate

Rest till
you're Ready.

Wear a watch or a chest strap and your heart rate rides along with every set. When you've recovered, the rest ring turns lime and swolie says go. No more guessing if the rest was long enough.

  • Live heart rate while you lift
  • Ready shows up on your Lock Screen too
  • Peak heart rate for every set, after the fact

Your plan

Always know
what's next.

Pick your days and swolie builds the week around them. Open the app and today's workout is already waiting, with every exercise, set and rep laid out.

  • 3, 4 and 6-day plans, or train flexibly
  • Rest days that stay rest days
  • Swap a workout when life happens
